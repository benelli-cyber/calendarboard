Calendars

To add a Google Calendar: on a computer, open Google Calendar → ⚙ Settings → click the calendar on the left → Integrate calendar → copy Secret address in iCal format. Saved addresses stay on the server and are only shown shortened here.

Color rules

Color events by words in their title. The first matching rule wins, so use ↑ ↓ to put the most important rules on top. Whole words, any capitalization; plurals count ("dinner" also matches "dinners"). All-day events keep the all-day color.
